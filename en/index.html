Tech Reports
Technical Reports

Systematic experiments combining feed-forward neural reconstruction (VGGT / VGGT-Ω / 3DGS) with classical geometric pipelines.

Bending Feed-forward Reconstruction into 3D Gaussian Splatting: One-shot Bundle Adjustment for VGGT / VGGT-Ω Pose and Intrinsics

Zong Wei · 2026 · Technical Report · bilingual abstract (中英)

Building on the route "VGGT one-shot forward + external 2D observations + one global BA", the gap is fully attributed to network poses & intrinsics, not the initial point cloud. On an identically configured 83-frame matrix (11 held-out views): reusing the SIFT 2D observations from the production pipeline, one-shot triangulation under frozen network poses + a global BA (with k₁ self-calibration) lifts VGGT-Ω held-out PSNR from 21.35 to 25.79 dB (protocol A) / 26.72 dB (protocol B), matching the full COLMAP pipeline (25.77 / 26.82 dB) with better LPIPS; VGGT recovers +5.12 dB. Three counter-intuitive conclusions are evidenced: feeding the network's dense point map back into 3DGS initialization actually hurts by 2.8–3.2 dB; self-supervised "self-proof" BA built from network-generated tracks reaches the lowest residual yet degrades the pose; and the full chain is validated on a domestic AI accelerator (Skydium BI-V150S, CoreX-5.0).
